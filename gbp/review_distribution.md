# Review distribution — how to actually get the reviews

The review funnel is live at **https://trishikacarrentalhubli.in/review/**
(scan/tap → pick stars → 4–5★ get a ready-to-post Google review, 1–3★ go privately to your WhatsApp).
It does nothing until customers reach it. This is how you feed it.

## The #1 move: WhatsApp after every drop-off
The single highest-converting moment is **within 15–30 minutes of dropping the customer**, from the
business WhatsApp (**+91 82175 77849**). They just rode with you, the phone is in their hand, done in 10 seconds.

**Make it a WhatsApp "Quick Reply"** (WhatsApp Business → Settings → Business tools → Quick replies →
add `/rate`) so the driver/office sends it in two taps.

## Second channel: the QR card in every car
Print the **review QR card** (`review/qr/` → also a print-ready card artifact) and:
- Slip one into the **seat-back pocket** / hand it with the receipt.
- Stick a small one near the **rear door handle** ("Enjoyed the ride? Scan to rate ⭐").
- Driver line at drop-off: *"Sir/madam, if the ride was good, please scan this and give us a rating —
  it takes 10 seconds and helps us a lot."*

## Do / Don't (keep the reviews from getting stripped)
- ✅ Ask **everyone** who had a good trip — steady, natural flow beats a burst.
- ✅ Send from the **business number**, personal tone, one message per customer.
- ❌ **Never** offer a discount / cashback / free ride for a review (against Google + FTC — reviews get removed, listing can be penalised).
- ❌ Don't ask them to "give 5 stars" — just ask them to rate; the funnel handles the rest.
- ❌ Don't blast the same customer repeatedly — one ask + at most one follow-up.
