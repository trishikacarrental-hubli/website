## Rules (compliance — protects the 5.0)
- **Never** offer discounts/cash for reviews (violates Google policy; removal + suspension risk).
- **Never** gate ("only ask happy customers via a filter app") — also a violation. Ask everyone; the product is good.
- Never write reviews yourselves/family from the office WiFi. Velocity should track actual trip volume.
- Reply to **every** review within 48h (owner responses are a ranking + conversion signal).
