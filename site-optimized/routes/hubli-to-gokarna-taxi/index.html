Route facts

The Hubli–Gokarna run,
from drivers who do it weekly

— 01

The route

Hubli → Yellapur → Ankola → NH-66 south to Gokarna. Forest ghats till Ankola, then the coast road.

— 02

Best departure time

Out by 6 AM: temple darshan before the queue, beach by afternoon, sunset at Om Beach, home by night.

— 03

Temple etiquette

Mahabaleshwar Temple has a dress code (traditional preferred). The driver drops you at the temple street — vehicles stop short of the inner lanes.

— 04

The beaches

Om, Kudle, Half Moon and Paradise. Om Beach has road access + parking; Half Moon and Paradise are boat/trek only.

— 05

Combine with Murudeshwar

Another ~55 km south — the giant Shiva statue plus Gokarna in one long, brilliant day. Tell us in advance and we plan the timing.

— 06

Overnight option

Beach stays are cheap on weekdays. Two-day trips bill the 300 km/day minimum with the driver on batta.
